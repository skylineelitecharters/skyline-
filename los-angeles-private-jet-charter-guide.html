Van Nuys Airport is a major private aviation gateway, although the best airport depends on your itinerary.

Can I fly privately from Los Angeles to Las Vegas?

Yes. Los Angeles to Las Vegas is a popular private aviation route, subject to availability.

Can private jets be used for business travel?
